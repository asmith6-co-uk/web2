Notion Health Sync

Notion Health Sync is a private, personal automation used by its owner to copy selected health and fitness data from Google Health into the owner's private Notion database.

The service is not offered to the public and does not provide accounts, subscriptions, advertising, or commercial services.

Data accessed may include activity and fitness metrics, health measurements, sleep, nutrition, and hydration information. The data is used only to create the owner's personal daily health records in Notion.
