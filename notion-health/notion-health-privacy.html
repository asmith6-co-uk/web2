Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

About Notion Health Sync

Notion Health Sync is a private, personal automation used only by its owner. It connects the owner's Google Health data to the owner's private Notion database. It is not a public or commercial service.

Google user data accessed

The automation may request read-only access to selected Google Health data, including activity and fitness metrics, health measurements, sleep, nutrition, and hydration data.

How the data is used

Google Health data is used only to create and update the owner's personal daily health records in the owner's private Notion workspace.

Data storage

Selected health metrics are stored in the owner's private Notion database. OAuth credentials or tokens required for the automation are stored within the owner's private Google Apps Script environment and are not intentionally exposed publicly.

Data sharing

Google user data is not sold, rented, used for advertising, or shared with third parties for independent purposes. Data is transferred only as necessary between Google Health, Google Apps Script, and the owner's private Notion workspace to operate the automation.

Data retention and deletion

The owner controls the data stored in Notion and may delete it at any time. Access to Google Health data can also be revoked at any time through the owner's Google Account permissions.

Limited use

Use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Contact

This automation is for private personal use. Any questions about this privacy policy should be directed to the site owner using the contact details associated with this website.
